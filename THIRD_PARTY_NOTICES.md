# Third-party acknowledgements for the storybook

The website's existing page-turn engine is [StPageFlip](https://github.com/Nodlik/StPageFlip), distributed under the MIT License and loaded as a pinned browser bundle. The storybook's layout, text, audio and navigation remain the existing project.

The flower garden in `index.html` and `storybook-extras.css` adapts the layered petal, stem-growth, staggered bloom and floating-light approach of [FIQTOR/flowers-for-someone](https://github.com/FIQTOR/flowers-for-someone). The garden has been redrawn and styled for the book, without copying its full-screen page, letter text or unrelated assets.

The scratch card interaction in `storybook-extras.js` adapts the canvas `destination-out` erasure approach of [TH3-S4URAV/pookie-birthday-surprise](https://github.com/TH3-S4URAV/pookie-birthday-surprise). The miniature letters are inspired by that project's "Open When" collection but use new in-page envelope interactions rather than its modal component.

Both projects are MIT licensed. Required copyright and permission notices are reproduced below.
